# Account settings

Open workspace Settings and use the Account section for usage, billing, seats, and advanced organization controls. Treat amounts, plans, invoices, members, and access state as private live data.

## Usage

Use Usage for read-only reporting. It supports period and workspace scope selection plus views by user, project, or resource. Do not repeat private usage details unless the user asks.

## Billing

Billing can expose plans, payment methods, credits, invoices, subscription management, and cancellation. Inspect without changing state. Obtain confirmation immediately before purchasing credits, changing a payment method or plan, managing a subscription, or opening a flow that can lead to a financial commitment.

## Account seats

Account seats cover collaborators across workspaces, with users, roles, viewers, invites, search, export, and member actions. Treat member and invite data as private. Obtain confirmation before inviting, changing roles, removing members, exporting, or changing seats.

## Advanced

Advanced controls may require an enterprise plan. They can cover SAML SSO, SCIM, audit logs, dependency-protection policy, publishing and privacy policy, attachment and export restrictions, model-provider availability, and deployment geography.

Treat every advanced change as high impact. Explain the account-wide or workspace-wide scope and obtain confirmation immediately before changing it. Do not follow a contact-sales flow unless the user explicitly asks to initiate it.
