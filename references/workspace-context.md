# Workspace Context

Treat a Replit workspace as the operating boundary for projects, conversations, data, members, settings, integrations, and permissions.

## Before acting

1. Identify the selected workspace in the navigation.
2. Confirm it matches the user's intended personal or team workspace.
3. Check that the requested action is permitted there.
4. Treat workspace-specific administration, billing, settings, and integrations as separate from personal account preferences.

## Access expectations

- Personal projects are intended for the individual workspace owner.
- Team-workspace projects can be accessible to the team, subject to the workspace's membership and permission configuration.
- Do not infer access rights from a project name or browser history. Verify the intended workspace and observable permission state.

## Evidence

Record the displayed workspace name and the observable state supporting the requested action. Do not record or expose sensitive account identifiers unless needed and approved.
